# Module 1 Extension Walkthrough - Urban Heat Hotspot Engine

Extended Module 1 of the Boreas-Nexus pipeline with three essential capabilities:

1. **Hotspot Cluster Metadata**
2. **City Temperature Percentile**
3. **Hotspot Confidence Score**

All changes keep the **Urban Heat Hotspot Knowledge Layer** stored in **GeoParquet** format (`urban_heat_hotspot_knowledge_layer.geoparquet` / `urban_heat_hotspots.geoparquet`) as the single source of truth.

---

## 1. Accomplished Objectives

### Extension 1: Hotspot Cluster Metadata (`HotspotClusterGenerator`)

- Connected component analysis using `scipy.ndimage.label` with configurable 4- and 8-neighbour connectivity (`config/hotspot_scoring.yaml`).
- Unique cluster ID assignment (`HOT_0001`, `HOT_0002`, ...). Non-hotspot points have `hotspot_id = null`.
- Morphological and thermal metric calculations: `cluster_area_m2`, `cluster_perimeter_m`, `cluster_size_pixels`, `cluster_centroid`, `cluster_bbox`, `mean_lst`, `peak_lst`, `mean_suhii`, `mean_heat_persistence`, and `mean_hotspot_confidence_score`.
- Vector cluster polygon generation exported to `data/processed/hotspot_clusters.geojson` and `data/processed/hotspot_clusters.gpkg`.

### Extension 2: City Temperature Percentile (`CityTemperaturePercentileCalculator`)

- Day LST percentile ranking within the study area ignoring NoData and Water (`is_water == True`).
- Computes `city_temperature_percentile` (0.0 to 100.0), `temperature_rank`, and `temperature_total_pixels`.

### Extension 3: Hotspot Confidence Score (`HotspotConfidenceScorer`)

- Deterministic weighted scoring model (0–100) combining:
    - Getis-Ord Gi* Z-score (45%)
    - Surface Urban Heat Island Intensity - SUHII (30%)
    - Heat Persistence Index (15%)
    - City Temperature Percentile (10%)
- Configured via [`config/hotspot_scoring.yaml`](file:///d:/Projects/Boreas_Nexus/config/hotspot_scoring.yaml).
- Maps scores to confidence classifications: `Critical`, `Very High`, `High`, `Moderate`, `Low`, and `Very Low`.

### Analytics-First Storage Architecture & Reports

- **Primary GeoParquet Knowledge Layer**: `data/processed/urban_heat_hotspots.geoparquet` & `data/processed/urban_heat_hotspot_knowledge_layer.geoparquet`.
- **Normalized Hotspot Registry**: `data/processed/hotspot_registry.parquet`.
- **Validation Reports**: `data/metadata/cluster_validation.json` & `data/metadata/metadata.json`.
- **Optional GIS Products**: `urban_heat_hotspots.geojson`, `urban_heat_hotspots.gpkg`, `hotspot_clusters.geojson`, `hotspot_clusters.gpkg`.
